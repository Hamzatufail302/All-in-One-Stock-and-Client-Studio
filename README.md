# All-In-One Stock & Client Studio 🚀
### Complete Creator Intelligence: Stock Marketplaces & Direct Client Acquisition Suite

**All-In-One Stock & Client Studio** is an all-in-one local intelligence platform built for digital creators, designers, and indie developers. It unites two high-earning pillars into a single command center:
1. **Stock Market Intelligence**: Live buyer demand, search trends, competition analysis, and prompt generation for **Adobe Stock, Shutterstock, Etsy, IconScout, and GraphicRiver**.
2. **Direct Client Acquisition Engine**: Real-world outreach pipeline for indie app developers, mobile studios, and creative agencies needing bespoke icons, UI kits, vector graphics, and stock media.

---

## 🌟 Key Modules & Capabilities

### 1. 🌐 Combined vs. Platform-Isolated Stock Search
- **Combined Search & Trends Hub**: Unified search across all marketplaces simultaneously with cross-platform comparison and calculated Opportunity Scores.
- **Dedicated Marketplace Tabs**: Independent tabs for **Adobe Stock, Shutterstock, Etsy, IconScout, and GraphicRiver**.
  - **In-Tab Search Engine**: Search exclusively within that marketplace's buyer index.
  - **Format-Isolated Specs**: Separate guidance for 📐 **Vectors**, 📸 **Images / Photos / AI**, and 🎬 **4K Stock Video**.
  - **AI Acceptance Policy Callouts**: Real-world platform rules (e.g. Adobe Stock 100% accepts AI images & video; Shutterstock restricts external third-party AI; Etsy demand for clipart and digital templates).
  - **"What to Create" Concepts**: High-conversion concepts with ready-to-copy titles and tags.

### 2. 💼 Direct Client Acquisition & Outreach CRM
- **Indie App & Developer Outreach**: Target indie developers building Android and cross-platform apps needing app icons, splash screens, onboarding illustrations, and UI graphics.
- **Digital Agency & Game Studio Leads**: Pre-qualified client segments seeking custom asset packages.
- **One-Click Pitch Generator**: Generates customized, high-converting cold pitches based on client niche, app category, and asset requirements.
- **Built-in Outreach CRM**: Track outreach status (`Identified` ➔ `Contacted` ➔ `Follow-Up` ➔ `Won`) with notes and contact details stored permanently in SQLite.

### 3. ⚡ Daily Production Tracker & Goal Manager
- **Daily Quotas**: Set daily targets for Vectors, AI Images, AI Videos, and Canva/PSD Templates.
- **One-Click Asset Logging**: Click "+ Log Done" from any search result, AI prompt card, or event to add to today's count.
- **Persistent Local Database**: All logged assets, completion percentages, notes, and targets are saved permanently in [`stock_crawler.db`](file:///c:/Users/Hamza%20Tufail/Documents/keyword%20cwarler%20for%20stock/data/stock_crawler.db).
- **Streak & Consistency Radar**: 14-day consistency tracker with fire streak badge (`🔥 X Day Streak`).
- **Pipeline Status**: Track assets from `Created` to `Uploaded`.

### 4. 🤖 Commercial AI Prompt Generator (15–20 Prompts/Day)
- Tailored for **Google Flow, Midjourney v6, Flux.1, Runway Gen-3, Luma Dream Machine, and Sora**.
- Generates batches of **20 ready-to-run commercial prompts**:
  - **10 Commercial AI Stock Photos / Backgrounds** with lighting, camera lenses (e.g. Hasselblad H6D-100c, 85mm f/1.8), color grading, and negative copy space.
  - **10 4K Stock Video B-Roll Loops** with camera movement (gimbal dolly, aerial drone descent, 60fps slow-motion loops, ProRes 422).
- Each prompt includes: Ready Stock Title, ready copy tags, aspect ratio, and "+ Log to Today" button.

### 5. 📅 365-Day Daily Ranked Events & Calendar
- Dynamically scans global holidays, shopping milestones, and cultural dates starting from today forward.
- **5-Star Commercial Ranking (⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐)**: Ranked by earning potential and lead time.
- **60–90 Day Production Lead Times**: Alerts you when to create assets (e.g. Christmas, New Year, Black Friday, Valentine's Day) well ahead of peak buying surges.
- Separated asset recommendations for **Illustrator Vectors, AI Images, 4K Videos, and Canva/PSD Templates** for every holiday.

### 6. 🔍 Multi-Engine Trend Crawler & Alphabet Soup
- **Direct Adobe Stock Autocomplete**: Real-time integration with Adobe's Fotolia backend for exact search popularity scores (`0.0 - 1.0`).
- **Alphabet-Soup Deep Digger (A–Z)**: Automatically cycles seed keywords through 26 letters (`seed + a`, `seed + b`, ...) to uncover **100 to 250+** high-converting long-tail buyer queries.
- **Tag Generators**: Ready-to-copy tags formatted for Adobe Stock (49 tags) and Etsy (13 tags).

### 7. 📋 Production Kanban Queue
- Organize ideas and design roadmaps across 4 stages: `Idea` ➔ `Designing` ➔ `Ready` ➔ `Uploaded`.
- Export your production roadmap or Alphabet Soup keywords to CSV.
